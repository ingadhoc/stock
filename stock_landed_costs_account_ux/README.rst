=============================
Stock Landed Costs Account UX
=============================

#. Adds the journal entries of the validated landed costs of a stock move to the journal entries button of the stock moves lists, next to its own entry, value adjustments and invoices.
#. The main journal entry of the move is left as is: a landed cost does not mark the move as booked.
